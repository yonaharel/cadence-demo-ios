A clean, fast guitar tuner. Play a string and Twang shows the note, how many cents sharp or flat it is, and which way to turn the peg.

• 14 tunings: Standard, Half step down, Whole step down, C Standard, Drop D, Drop C♯, Drop C, Double Drop D, Open D, Open E, Open G, Open A, Open C and DADGAD
• The string you're tuning is highlighted, and the app remembers your last tuning
• Tap any string to hear its note and tune by ear
• A steady needle with a clear in-tune zone
• Works offline; the microphone is only used while the tuner is open, and audio never leaves your iPhone
