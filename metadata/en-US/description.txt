A clean, fast guitar tuner. Play a string and the tuner shows the note, how many cents sharp or flat it is, and which way to turn the peg.

• 14 tunings: Standard, Half step down, Drop D, Open G, DADGAD and more. The tuner remembers your last choice
• The string you're tuning is highlighted; tap any string to hear its note
• A steady needle with a clear in-tune zone
• Works offline; the microphone is only used while the tuner is open, and audio never leaves your iPhone
