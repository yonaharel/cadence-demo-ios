PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Twang is a guitar tuner for guitarists of any level, from beginners to working musicians. Tuning by ear is hard, and an out-of-tune guitar sounds wrong however well it is played. Twang listens to a string through the microphone and shows the note, how many cents sharp or flat it is, and which way to turn the peg. It supports 14 common tunings (Standard, Drop D, Open G, DADGAD and more), and tapping a string plays its reference note so players can also tune by ear.

SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No account, login, sample files or setup is needed.
1. Launch the app and allow microphone access when asked. The microphone is used only while the app is in the foreground. Audio is analysed on the device and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
2. Play a guitar string near the iPhone. The large letter shows the note, the needle shows how far off it is, and the text says "Flat · tune up", "Sharp · tune down" or "In tune". The string being tuned is highlighted in the row at the top.
3. Pick a different tuning from the menu at the top (for example Drop D). The app remembers the last choice.
4. Tap any string in the row to hear its reference note.
No guitar? Humming, whistling, or playing a tone from another device (anything from about 60 to 1,200 Hz) works the same way.
If microphone access is denied, the app explains why it needs it and offers a button to open Settings.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app has no network access, no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Everything runs on the device and works offline.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in English.

REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected material. Note names and tuning names are standard music terms, and all sounds are generated by the app.
