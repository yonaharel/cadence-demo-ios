1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached: recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at app launch and shows the microphone permission prompt, tuning strings (flat, sharp, in tune), tapping a string to hear its reference note, and changing the tuning. The app has no accounts, no user-generated content and no paid content or in-app purchases, so it has no login, account deletion, reporting/blocking or purchase flows.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Twang is a chromatic guitar tuner for guitarists of any level, from beginners tuning for the first time to players switching between alternate tunings. Tuning by ear is hard and an out-of-tune guitar sounds wrong however well it is played. Twang listens to a plucked string through the microphone and shows the nearest note, how many cents sharp or flat it is on a needle, and whether to tune up or down, with a clear in-tune zone (within 5 cents). It supports 14 common tunings (Standard, Half/Whole step down, C Standard, Drop D, Drop C#, Drop C, Double Drop D, Open D/E/G/A/C, DADGAD), highlights the string being tuned, and plays any string's reference note when tapped, for tuning by ear. It works fully offline.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No login, account, setup or sample files are needed.
- Launch the app and allow microphone access when asked. The microphone is used only while the app is on screen and stops when it goes to the background.
- Play a guitar string. The note, its octave, frequency and cents offset appear, the matching string at the top is highlighted, and the text says "Flat · tune up", "Sharp · tune down" or "In tune".
- Without a guitar: sing or hum a steady note, or play a tone from another device (for example 110 Hz, the A string). The tuner detects pitches from about 60 Hz to 1200 Hz.
- Tap any of the six string buttons to hear that string's note for 2 seconds.
- Tap the tuning menu at the top to switch tunings. The choice is remembered across launches.
- If microphone access is denied, the app explains why it needs it and offers an "Open Settings" button.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests and has no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services. It uses only Apple's frameworks (SwiftUI, AVFoundation). Pitch detection runs on the device using the published YIN algorithm (de Cheveigné & Kawahara, 2002), implemented in the app's own code. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. It has no region-specific content or features, and because it never goes online, it behaves the same with or without a connection.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no licensed or protected third-party material: no music recordings, samples, artwork or trademarks. Note names and guitar tunings are standard public musical knowledge, and the reference tones are generated by the device.
